Connection map

The map shows the neighbourhood of one thought: its connections and theirs, at most 2 steps and 200 thoughts (proposed budget). Relations are solid lines with their type; links written in a note are dashed lines named "mentions" (Zettelkasten decision 2). Tapping a thought highlights its connections; tapping a highlighted thought moves the map there. The outline stays the main form; the map is a way to look across it. iPad first. Example content only.

Where the map lives on the iPad

On the phone the map is always its own place (frames 3 and 4). On the iPad the map is its own place with the selected thought at the side (D-168); on the phone a card stands in for the side (D-169). There is no visible list; screen readers get the map as a structured list (D-170). Zoom by pinch, drag and ＋ −, with names only where they fit (D-171), is not drawn. Frame 5 is decided provisionally (D-172); the table at the end holds proposals, not yet decided.
