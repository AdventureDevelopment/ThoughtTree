Gap G6 of the information architecture check. The item menu grew from 9 entries (D-59) to 14 with Copy, Paste below, Sort, Export…, Import into this thought… and the connection map. Two variants, each shown in the outline and on the ⋯ of the focused thought (D-166). Right hand, Paper theme, something is copied (so Paste below shows), "Offer feedback" is on (so the quiet last line shows). Example content only.

Thin lines separate the groups; Paste below shows only while something is copied. Everything is one level deep, but on a phone the list scrolls.

Variant D · in Focus, a button panel and More (owner's idea)

In Focus the thought's most used actions are always visible as a panel under the heading, and ▾ Actions opens the rest. It is not called More and has no ⋯, so it cannot be mixed up with ⋯ More in the bar, which holds the tree's actions (D-53). The panel grows out of the existing line (🕘 ↗ ↙, D-151), so Focus gets one row, not two. Tags need no button: the chips with ＋ are already there (D-64); Link to… sits in the Links sheet (＋ Link to…, D-85), shown even at 0 links. The outline keeps its popover (A or C).
