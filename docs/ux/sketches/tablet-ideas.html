Tablet and desktop: a desk, not an office

Other ideas for the wide screen. No panes, splitters or toolbars across the top: sheets of paper lie on a quiet desk, the actions float in a small dock, and the empty width is allowed to stay calm. Example content only.

1 · Margin notes

One sheet in the middle at a comfortable reading width. The selected thought's note, tags and links appear beside its row, in the margin, like notes pencilled into a book. Nothing opens, nothing splits.

3 · Unfold in place

One sheet. Opening a thought unfolds it right where it is: its note, tags and links appear under its row and push the rest down; folding it closes it again. You never leave the tree.

4 · Two sheets on the desk

Two places of the tree lie next to each other, for example your Ideas and the Inbox. You carry a thought from one sheet to the other by hand. Made for the quiet hour of giving thoughts their place; the second sheet is laid down and taken away as needed.
