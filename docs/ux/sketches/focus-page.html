Focus as a page: a heading is a thought

Focus shows everything a thought is: title, tags, note, and the thoughts inside it, formatted as headings with their own notes. Typing a heading in the note starts a new thought. Example content only.

Shortcuts are commands

@@, ::, ## and [[ are commands, interpreted while you type and shown as a proposal. Accepted, they become the action. A tag command leaves the text; a link keeps its anchor in the text and creates a relation.
