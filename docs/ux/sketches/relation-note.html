Relation note

A relation can carry a note: why the two thoughts are connected (ADR-077). This sketch shows where the reason is read and written, where a relation's type is changed (gap G2), and how a screen reader hears it. Example from acceptance scenario 2: "Spaced repetition" supports "Why we forget what we read" because it shows the same effect. Phone, Paper theme, example content only.

The reason sits under each relation in muted type, two lines at most, like the note preview in the outline (D-42). A relation without one shows "＋ Add a reason" on its own line in the same place, smaller and in the accent text colour (D-176); one tap opens the relation sheet (frame 3) with the field ready. A reason is the exception, so nothing asks for it. Each relation's ⋯ opens the same sheet.

One place for everything about one connection, read from left to right: from, type, to. The type chip opens the list of types (with their reverse names; "＋ New type…" at the end). The reason is written like a note: Markdown without headings, saved as you type, its own 🕘 history and undo (ADR-077). Remove is set apart; it is undone with Undo, and the reason comes back with the connection. ✕ or ✓ closes; nothing to save.

Linking, and the reason on the map (D-175)

Link to… opens the target picker (D-9, D-13). The new relation gets the type "related" until changed.

Selecting a thought on the map shows the reason of its line to the middle thought in the card (phone, D-169) or at the top of the side column (iPad, D-168); its ⋯ opens the relation sheet. The map draws no reason text on the lines; zoomed out they would not fit (D-171).

Tapping a line or its type label selects the connection: the line is drawn thicker in the accent colour, and the card (phone) or side column (iPad) shows from → type → to and the reason, or "＋ Add a reason" when there is none; ⋯ opens the relation sheet (frame 3). A dashed link shows the sentence around it instead, with no type or reason. The tap area of a line is at least 24 px wide (ADR-033). Keyboard and screen readers reach the same through the thought at its end (D-172).

Screen reader hears on a relation row: "Supported by Spaced repetition. Reason: Shows the same effect: what is repeated at the right time stays. Button: Type and reason." Without a reason: "Related to Writing as thinking. No reason. Button: Add a reason." On the map (D-172) the reason is read after the line's type.
