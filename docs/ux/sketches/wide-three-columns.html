Wide screens: three clean columns

One surface, hairlines instead of boxes, space and type instead of decoration. Tablet in landscape or a desktop window, Paper theme. Example content only.

1 · Three columns, details at the side

Navigation at one side (can be collapsed), the outline in the middle, the selected thought's details at the other side. The bar stays under the outline; 📥 keeps its corner.

2 · Details in the middle: reads like a book

The side column is gone; every thought's note unfolds under its title. Top-level thoughts read as chapter headings, the ones inside as sections, notes as paragraphs, at a comfortable reading width. The selected thought is softly tinted and shows its tags and links. Navigation is collapsed to a slim rail here.

Writing notes on the wide screen

The heavy writing happens here. The note is formatted as you type (Markdown shortcuts and the usual keys such as Ctrl+B work); a small format row appears only while a note is being written. Everything is saved as you type.

4 · Writing in the details column

Click into the note and write. The format row sits quietly above the note; "⤢ Give it room" opens the writing page (6) when the note outgrows the column.

5 · Writing in the middle, in the book

With details in the middle, you write right in the text: click a paragraph and type. Selecting words brings the format row up next to them; Enter in a title starts the next thought, Enter in a note starts a new paragraph.

6 · The writing page: one thought, nothing else

"⤢ Give it room" (or Open) shows the thought alone at a comfortable reading width: title, note, the format row, nothing around it. ← returns to exactly where you were. 📥 stays, so a passing thought can still be caught.
