Pairing and exchanging by file

Release 1 has no server: your own devices hold the same tree after pairing (once per tree and device), and then bring each other up to date by an encrypted file, as a round trip (D-19, D-20). This sketch shows both, phone first. "Phone" holds the tree "My thoughts"; "Tablet" is the new device. Example content only.
